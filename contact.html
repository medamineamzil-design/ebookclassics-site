Contact

A question, a mistake in a book, a download that does not work, a title you would like to see in the library? Write to us:

contact@bookclassics.org

We usually answer within a few days. To help us, mention the book title and the language of the edition.

Stop receiving emails

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can also write to us and we will remove your address.

Rights holders

All books offered are believed to be in the public domain. If you think a work or an image on BookClassics should not be here, write to us with the details and we will look into it promptly.
