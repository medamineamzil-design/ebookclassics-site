About the book

As Duke Theseus of Athens prepares to marry Hippolyta, queen of the Amazons, a young woman named Hermia is ordered by her father to marry Demetrius, though she loves Lysander. The two lovers flee into the forest, followed by Demetrius and by Helena, who loves him. In the same wood, Oberon and Titania, king and queen of the fairies, are quarrelling, and Oberon's mischievous servant Puck is armed with a magic flower whose juice makes the sleeper fall in love with the first creature seen on waking. Also in the forest is a group of Athenian craftsmen, among them the irrepressible weaver Nick Bottom, rehearsing a play for the Duke's wedding.

The comedy weaves its several plots together with great lightness. It plays with the madness of love, the power of imagination and the strange logic of dreams, and its scenes of the craftsmen's earnest, clumsy theatre are among the funniest Shakespeare wrote. The poetry ranges from courtly verse to fairy songs to deliberate doggerel, and the play remains a favourite for first-time readers and audiences of all ages.

The play is generally dated to around 1595 or 1596 and was first printed in 1600. It has been performed constantly and has inspired music, including Felix Mendelssohn's famous incidental score, as well as ballets, an opera by Benjamin Britten, and many films.

About William Shakespeare

William Shakespeare was born in Stratford-upon-Avon and baptised on 26 April 1564. His father was a glover and town official. At eighteen he married Anne Hathaway, and they had three children, including twins, Hamnet and Judith; Hamnet died at eleven. By the early 1590s Shakespeare was working in London as an actor and playwright. He became a shareholder in the Lord Chamberlain's Men, later the King's Men, and in the Globe Theatre. He later returned to Stratford, where he died in 1616.

Shakespeare wrote around thirty-seven plays, some in collaboration, as well as 154 sonnets and long narrative poems. They include comedies such as Twelfth Night, tragedies including Romeo and Juliet, Hamlet, Othello, King Lear and Macbeth, and late romances such as The Tempest. In 1623 two fellow actors collected his plays in the First Folio. His insight into human nature and inventive language make his works the most performed and studied in the world.

How it begins

OBERON. I pray thee give it me. I know a bank where the wild thyme blows, Where oxlips and the nodding violet grows, Quite over-canopied with luscious woodbine, With sweet musk-roses, and with eglantine. There sleeps Titania sometime of the night, Lull’d in these flowers with dances and delight; And there the snake throws her enamell’d skin, Weed wide enough to wrap a fairy in. And with the juice of this I’ll streak her eyes, And make her full of hateful fantasies. Take thou some of it, and seek through this grove: A sweet Athenian lady is in love With a disdainful youth. Anoint his eyes; But do it when the next thing he espies May be the lady. Thou shalt know the man By the Athenian garments he hath on. Effect it with some care, that he may prove More fond on her than she upon her love: And look thou meet me ere the first cock crow.

PUCK. Fear not, my lord, your servant shall do so.

[Exeunt.]

Scene II. Another part of the wood

Enter Titania with her Train.

TITANIA. Come, now a roundel and a fairy song; Then for the third part of a minute, hence; Some to kill cankers in the musk-rose buds; Some war with reremice for their leathern wings, To make my small elves coats; and some keep back The clamorous owl, that nightly hoots and wonders At our quaint spirits. Sing me now asleep; Then to your offices, and let me rest.

Fairies sing.

FIRST FAIRY. You spotted snakes with double tongue, Thorny hedgehogs, be not seen; Newts and blind-worms do no wrong, Come not near our Fairy Queen:

CHORUS. Philomel, with melody, Sing in our sweet lullaby: Lulla, lulla, lullaby; lulla, lulla, lullaby. Never harm, nor spell, nor charm, Come our lovely lady nigh; So good night, with lullaby.

FIRST FAIRY. Weaving spiders, come not here; Hence, you long-legg’d spinners, hence. Beetles black, approach not near; Worm nor snail do no offence.

CHORUS. Philomel with melody, &c.

SECOND FAIRY. Hence away! Now all is well. One aloof stand sentinel.
